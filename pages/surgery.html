Surgery basics

Understanding MPFL surgery

MPFL surgery helps stabilize the kneecap after repeated dislocations. Depending on your case, your surgeon may repair the ligament or reconstruct it.

Before-surgery checklist

01

Questions for your surgeon

Ask about pain management, brace use, weight-bearing rules, and return-to-sports milestones.

02

Home setup

Prepare a recovery station with meds, water, a charger, pillows, and easy-to-reach snacks.

03

School plan

Tell teachers and counselors ahead of time so attendance and assignments stay manageable.

04

Mental prep

Feeling nervous is normal. A clear plan reduces fear and gives you more control.

Surgery day, honestly

Bring your ID, comfortable clothes, and any required forms. After surgery, you may feel groggy and sore.

Keep communication open with your family and care team, and follow your discharge instructions carefully.
