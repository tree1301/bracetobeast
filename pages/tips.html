Mindset and practical tips

Things I wish I knew earlier

Recovery includes physical work and emotional work. These tips helped me when I felt stuck, discouraged, or impatient.

Daily tips

01

Track progress weekly

Write down one win each week. Tiny gains are easy to miss if you don’t record them.

02

Ask for help early

Lean on your support system before stress builds. Talking about it lowers the pressure.

03

Fuel your recovery

Stay hydrated, eat enough protein, and sleep as consistently as you can.

04

Protect your mind

Limit comparison. Social media rarely shows the hard parts of healing.

Journaling prompts

  1. What is one thing my knee can do this week that it couldn’t last week?
  2. What made today harder than I expected, and what helped, even a little?
  3. Who could I ask for help this week, and what would I ask them for?
  4. What am I looking forward to doing again once I’m cleared?
