After surgery

The recovery period

The first few weeks can feel slow and frustrating. This phase is about protecting your knee, reducing swelling, and building safe habits.

Weeks 1 to 4: what to focus on

01

Pain and swelling

Ice, elevation, a medication schedule, and rest are your best friends early on.

02

Safe movement

Use crutches and your brace exactly as instructed. Rushing too soon can delay progress.

03

Sleep and energy

Sleep may be uncomfortable at first. Keep a pillow setup that supports your leg.

04

School routine

Plan extra time between classes and ask for practical accommodations.

My honest advice

You might feel behind when friends continue normal activities. That feeling is valid. Recovery is not a competition. Following your plan now gives you the best chance at long-term strength.
