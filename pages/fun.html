Fun challenge ideas

01

Playlist challenge

Make a therapy playlist with one hype song, one calm song, and one throwback.

02

Sticker progress chart

Add a sticker for each week you stay consistent. Seeing progress feels satisfying.

03

Photo mini diary

Take one photo each week to track progress and look back at how far you’ve come.

Victory list
