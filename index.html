Quick questions

Things people ask me

Short answers from my own experience. Your care team always has the final word.

Will I feel normal right away?

No. Healing happens in layers. Most people need months of gradual progress, not days.

What if I miss sports or activities?

That feeling is real. I replaced “what I can’t do” with small weekly goals, and that helped a lot.

How do I handle school?

I emailed my teachers early and asked for temporary flexibility, so I could recover without panicking about assignments.

When can I go back to my sport?

Your therapist and surgeon decide when you’re ready, based on strength and function, not on pressure or a date on the calendar.
